# CommandLane Community Code of Conduct

We are building CommandLane for power users and teams who care about focus,
privacy, and craft. Our community spaces (GitHub, Discord, etc.) should reflect
those values.

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions related to this
repository and associated discussion channels.

## Our standards

We strive to foster a community that is:

- **Respectful**: Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **Inclusive**: No harassment or discrimination based on personal
  characteristics or background.
- **Constructive**: Critique should be specific, actionable, and kind.
- **Security-conscious**: No sharing of sensitive data, credentials, or
  exploit details in public.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Offering thoughtful, respectful feedback
- Helping others understand design trade-offs
- Being patient with newcomers

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Sharing non-public vulnerabilities or attack details publicly

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

- Email: **support@commandlane.ai**

We take reports seriously and will review them promptly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including but not
limited to:

- Asking a participant to stop or edit their content
- Temporarily or permanently restricting participation in community spaces
- Escalating to platform moderation tools (GitHub, Discord, etc.)

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all CommandLane community spaces and when
an individual is representing the project or community in public channels.
